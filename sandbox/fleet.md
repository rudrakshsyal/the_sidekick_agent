# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E for a 100-Car Sales Fleet

## Quick comparison

| Criterion | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---|---|---|
| EPA range | ~294–357 miles | ~250–320 miles |
| Seating / cargo | 5 seats; ~30 cu ft rear cargo, up to ~74 cu ft max | 5 seats; ~29.7 cu ft rear cargo, up to ~59.7 cu ft max |
| Charging | Tesla Supercharger access; strong route planning | DC fast charging; newer models get NACS adapter access |
| Driver tech | Strong software/telematics; Autopilot/FSD extra | BlueCruise hands-free highway driving; subscriptions may apply |
| Cost / ops | Higher variability in insurance, tires, and service logistics | Lower starting MSRP; similar EV savings, but insurance/tire wear still matter |
| Fleet risk | Repair/service can be harder without dealer network | Conventional Ford service network is a practical advantage |

## Recommendation

**Recommend the Ford Mustang Mach-E for the 100-car sales fleet.**

Reason: it offers adequate range, usable cargo space, and a more conventional service and support model that is easier to manage at fleet scale. The Tesla Model Y has stronger charging convenience and slightly better range, but the Mach-E is the safer operational choice for a large sales fleet where downtime, service access, and predictable support matter more than peak charging/network advantage.
